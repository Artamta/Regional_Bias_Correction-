## Prohibited language

- “All models are bad,” “first India S2S benchmark,” “state of the art,” or
  universal FuXi/neural superiority.
- Appending ERPAS, CNRM, or Spire to the fixed 2020--2024 main ranking, or
  describing the limited-period ERPAS sensitivity as a common-initialization
  comparison.
- Any `resultsv2/` probabilistic calibration score, including its former
  2022--2024 audit and categorical comparison.
- Any continuous calibrated value not traceable to supported, immutable
  P02--P09 evidence. C01 values are separately permitted only as
  development-only NIPC-RPS/NIPC-RPSS under its explicit nonconventional
  scoring contract.
- Any claim that 2025 was evaluated, or any prospective/generalization claim.
- “Bias correction” as a blanket success unless signed bias and its interval
  pass the frozen gate.
